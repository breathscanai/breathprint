This is not a diagnosis. It's a pilot study with 15 volunteers. The AI was right 82% of the time for Type 2 diabetes vs. healthy and 70% for asthma vs. healthy, tested on people it had never seen. Only a doctor can test for these conditions.

How BreathScan works

1BreatheInto a clean, single-use mouthpiece on the sealed box.
2Sense4 gas sensors take 9 readings every second.
3MeasureAn Arduino finds how much each breath changed every gas.
4CompareAI trained on 15 volunteers looks for the matching pattern.
15adult volunteers, IRB approved with signed consent
82%correct, Type 2 diabetes vs. healthy (guessing: 55%)
70%correct, asthma vs. healthy (guessing: 60%)
$150or less to build. No needles, no lab.
